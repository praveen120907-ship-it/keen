Before anything else

My Keerthika,

Before the candles and the songs, I wanted to say something quieter. There were days I got it wrong — moments I was careless with your feelings when I should have been gentle, times I was distant when you needed me close. I'm not writing this so you'll say it's okay. I'm writing it because you deserve to hear it plainly: I see it, I own it, and I'm sorry.

You have never once made me feel small for admitting a mistake, and that kindness is one of the many reasons I love you. So on your day, the first thing I want you to have isn't a gift — it's this: my promise to keep learning how to love you better, one ordinary day at a time.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for being you.

— always yours
